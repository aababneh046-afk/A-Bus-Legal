A-Bus Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

A-Bus is a bus transportation application. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use A-Bus.

Information that may be processed

  • Account and contact information: phone number and information you provide when creating or managing an account.
  • Passenger and trip information: route selections, pickup requests, trip activity and related service communications.
  • Location information: location data when you use location-based features, such as finding a pickup point or sharing a driver's live bus location. Device permissions control access to location.
  • Driver application information: details and documents submitted by people applying to become drivers. These materials are intended for restricted access by authorized personnel.
  • Technical information: diagnostic, device and service-usage information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the application.

How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, verify phone-number control, manage passenger and driver services, coordinate routes and pickup requests, provide live transportation features, communicate about the service, prevent misuse, and maintain service security.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers that support the application, such as hosting, database, authentication, maps or messaging providers, where those services are used. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect users and the service. A-Bus does not offer personal information for sale.

Location and permissions

Location is relevant to certain transportation features. You can manage location permission through your device settings. Disabling permission may prevent some location-dependent features from working.

Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal, safety and operational requirements. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting us. See the Data Deletion Instructions.

Children

A-Bus is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as A-Bus develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
